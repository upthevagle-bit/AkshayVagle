The Classics
Time-honoured recipes every home bar should know
Old Fashioned
Bourbon Easy 3 min
Ingredients
60 mlBourbon whiskey
1 tspDemerara sugar syrup
2 dashesAngostura bitters
1 dashOrange bitters
1 largeIce cube
GarnishOrange peel twist
Method
1.Add sugar syrup and both bitters to a rocks glass and stir briefly to combine.
2.Add the large ice cube, then pour bourbon over.
3.Stir gently for 20–30 seconds until well chilled.
4.Express orange peel over the glass and place on the rim.
"The drink that never needs an introduction — just ice, bourbon, and an unwavering point of view."
Negroni
Gin Easy 3 min
Ingredients
30 mlLondon Dry Gin
30 mlCampari
30 mlSweet vermouth
IceCubed, for stirring
GarnishOrange slice or peel
Method
1.Combine gin, Campari and vermouth in a mixing glass with ice.
2.Stir for 30 seconds — smooth and cold is the goal.
3.Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube.
4.Garnish with an orange slice or twisted peel.
"Equal parts bold, bitter and beautiful — the cocktail that rewards those who commit."
Daiquiri
Rum Easy 4 min
Ingredients
60 mlWhite rum
30 mlFresh lime juice
20 mlSimple syrup (2:1)
IceFor shaking
GarnishLime wheel
Method
1.Add rum, lime juice and syrup to a shaker with ice.
2.Shake hard for 12–15 seconds until very cold.
3.Double-strain into a chilled coupe glass.
4.Garnish with a lime wheel on the rim.
"Three ingredients, zero compromise — proof that restraint is its own kind of luxury."

Margarita
Tequila Easy 4 min
Ingredients
50 mlBlanco tequila
25 mlTriple sec / Cointreau
25 mlFresh lime juice
½ tspAgave syrup
IceFor shaking
GarnishSalt rim + lime wedge
Method
1.Salt the rim of a chilled glass using a lime wedge and flaky salt.
2.Shake all liquid ingredients with ice for 15 seconds.
3.Double-strain into the prepared glass over fresh ice.
4.Add a lime wedge on the rim to finish.
"The cocktail that turns a Tuesday evening into a celebration worth remembering."
Espresso Martini
Vodka Easy 5 min
Ingredients
40 mlVodka
30 mlCoffee liqueur (Kahlúa)
30 mlFreshly pulled espresso
5 mlSimple syrup
IceFor shaking
Garnish3 coffee beans
Method
1.Pull your espresso and let it cool for 2 minutes.
2.Add all ingredients to a shaker with plenty of ice.
3.Shake vigorously for 15 seconds to build foam.
4.Double-strain into a chilled martini glass. Top with 3 coffee beans.
"When dessert and a nightcap collide — rich, velvety and completely impossible to say no to."
Aperol Spritz
Aperol Easy 2 min
Ingredients
90 mlProsecco
60 mlAperol
30 mlSoda water (splash)
IceCubed, in glass
GarnishOrange slice + green olive
Method
1.Fill a large wine glass with ice cubes.
2.Pour in prosecco first, then add Aperol.
3.Add a splash of soda and stir very gently once.
4.Garnish with an orange slice and an olive.
"Golden hour in a glass — the drink that makes any gathering feel like a European holiday."

Whiskey Sour
Whiskey Easy 5 min
Ingredients
50 mlBourbon or rye whiskey
30 mlFresh lemon juice
20 mlSimple syrup
1Egg white (optional)
GarnishLemon wheel + cherry
Method
1.If using egg white, dry shake all ingredients first (no ice) for 10 seconds.
2.Add ice and shake hard for 12–15 seconds.
3.Strain over a large ice cube in a rocks glass.
4.Garnish with a lemon wheel and cherry.
"Tart, silky and utterly satisfying — the cocktail that makes whiskey lovers out of everyone."
Manhattan
Rye Whiskey Easy 3 min
Ingredients
50 mlRye or bourbon whiskey
25 mlSweet vermouth
2 dashesAngostura bitters
IceFor stirring
GarnishMaraschino cherry
Method
1.Combine whiskey, vermouth and bitters in a mixing glass with ice.
2.Stir for 30 seconds until deeply chilled and slightly diluted.
3.Strain into a chilled coupe or martini glass.
4.Garnish with a maraschino cherry on a pick.
"New York's greatest contribution to cocktail culture — brooding, complex and absolutely non-negotiable."
Penicillin
Scotch Medium 6 min
Ingredients
50 mlBlended Scotch
15 mlIslay Scotch (float)
25 mlFresh lemon juice
20 mlHoney-ginger syrup
GarnishCandied ginger piece
Method
1.Shake blended Scotch, lemon and honey-ginger syrup hard with ice.
2.Strain into a rocks glass over one large ice cube.
3.Float Islay Scotch gently over the back of a spoon.
4.Garnish with a piece of candied ginger on a pick.
"Smoky, honeyed and bright — the modern cocktail that makes Scotch drinkers and converts in a single sip."
The Liquor Hub

Elevated Gin & Tonic
Gin Easy 2 min
Ingredients
45 mlPremium botanical gin
120 mlPremium tonic water
2 slicesCucumber
1 sprigFresh rosemary
IceLarge cubed
GarnishLime wheel + juniper
Method
1.Fill a highball glass with large ice cubes.
2.Add cucumber slices and bruised rosemary, then pour gin over.
3.Top with chilled premium tonic, pouring slowly down the side.
4.Stir once gently, garnish with lime wheel and a juniper sprig.
"A garden in a glass — the two-ingredient classic, elevated to something you'll actually want to photograph."
Paloma
Tequila Easy 3 min
Ingredients
50 mlBlanco tequila
20 mlFresh grapefruit juice
15 mlFresh lime juice
10 mlAgave syrup
60 mlGrapefruit soda (top)
GarnishSalt rim + grapefruit wedge
Method
1.Salt-rim a highball glass using a grapefruit wedge and flaky salt.
2.Fill with ice, then pour tequila, juices and agave over.
3.Stir briefly, then top with grapefruit soda.
4.Garnish with a grapefruit wedge and serve immediately.
"Mexico's favourite cocktail is tart, fizzy and impossibly refreshing — the Margarita's cooler sibling."
French 75
Gin + Champagne Easy 4 min
Ingredients
30 mlLondon Dry Gin
15 mlFresh lemon juice
10 mlSimple syrup
90 mlChampagne or Prosecco
IceFor shaking
GarnishLemon twist
Method
1.Shake gin, lemon juice and syrup with ice for 12 seconds.
2.Fine-strain into a chilled champagne flute.
3.Top slowly with chilled champagne or prosecco.
4.Express a lemon twist over the glass and drop it in.
"Effortlessly elegant and undeniably festive — the cocktail that makes every occasion feel like a celebration."
